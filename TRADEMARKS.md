# The Agreemetry name and logo

The [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) gives you broad rights to use, change and share the code. It does not grant rights to the Agreemetry name or logo (section 6 of the licence). This policy says how you may use them. Its aim is simple: people should be able to tell whether they are using the project's own software and service.

## Always fine

- Saying that your software or service is *based on*, *uses* or *runs* Agreemetry.
- Referring to Agreemetry in papers, teaching, talks, reviews and comparisons, including citing it.
- Running an unmodified release for your lab, department or institution and calling it Agreemetry, as long as you do not suggest that the project runs it.
- Distributing unmodified copies of the project's releases.

## Please ask first

- Using the name or logo in the name of your own product, service, company or domain.
- Using the logo on merchandise.

## Not allowed

- Calling a modified version *Agreemetry*, or presenting it as the official software or service. Rename a fork and keep a note that it is derived from Agreemetry.
- Suggesting endorsement by the project when there is none.

Questions and requests: open an issue, or contact the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
