# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, make releases, and decide the project's direction. They are listed below.
- **Contributors** are everyone who files issues, improves documentation, or proposes code.

| Maintainer | Role |
|---|---|
| [@tiamole](https://github.com/tiamole) | Founder and lead maintainer |

## Decisions

Most decisions are made in issues and pull requests by rough consensus among maintainers, after hearing contributors. Changes that affect blinding, the statistics, data formats or the database schema need a maintainer's explicit approval and a written rationale in the pull request. When consensus is not reached, the lead maintainer decides and records the reason.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, careful contributions and reviewed others' work may be invited by the existing maintainers. Maintainers who are inactive for a year may be moved to emeritus status.

## The name and the project's assets

The Agreemetry name, logo, domains and the GitHub organization are held for the project by the lead maintainer, to be used in the project's interest. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md). If the project later joins a fiscal host or foundation, these assets move with it.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are proposed by pull request and need the approval of the lead maintainer.
